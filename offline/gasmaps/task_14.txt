
HOW TO READ THIS MAP
  Rn[a-b]   = REGION id n, spanning rows a-b on that sheet. NOT R1C1 notation.
  R[-2]C[1] = R1C1 formula notation: row/col offsets from the cell itself.
  @         = a reference blanked out, leaving the formula's SHAPE (its skeleton).
  Blocks group by skeleton: one line can stand for thousands of cells.
  Difficulty tracks SKELETON COUNT in what you must write, not cell count.
  Every layer partitions the same grid independently; they may disagree.
  This map OBSERVES, it never concludes. Read cells directly before relying on them.
  Absent by construction: cell borders (no getBorder in Apps Script), merged ranges.
