Review the workbook carefully, then fill out the Forward 12-Month Projection section of the Forecast Model tab using the information provided in the rest of the workbook. All of your output should only be placed in columns H thru T within the Forward 12-Month Projection section. Do not change any existing data. Expenses on the Statement of Operations should be displayed as positive numbers including interest and income taxes. For the Statement of Cash Flows, anything that indicates a cash outflow for the firm should be displayed as a negative number. For working capital items, you should assume a monthly balance of 0.  For Service and Product Costs, look for Services — Total and Products — Total in the workbook, and look to the Scenario Planning section for financing cash flow considerations.
